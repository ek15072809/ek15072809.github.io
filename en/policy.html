Usage Policy

01

Using Our Projects

Unless otherwise stated, the projects of ek15072809 and Tema_Q LLM are free to use. When forking or reusing them, please act with restraint. We would also be glad if you could support the idea of avant-garde, unrestricted, open LLMs.

02

On Licensing

Many of the models published under Tema_Q LLM / TaQuants are fine-tuned, merged, or quantized versions based on publicly available models. When redistributing or using them, please follow the license terms of the base model listed on each model card.

For code in repositories (GitHub), the LICENSE file in each repository is authoritative. If none is provided, please contact the author before use.

Models, repositories, technical reports, and similar materials are released under the MIT License, except where another license takes precedence. If you use them in research or projects, please cite them along with the relevant reference.

03

On Responsible Use

Tema_Q's models are tuned to generate freer output, even for prompts that standard models tend to resist responding to. This characteristic is primarily intended for creative and research purposes, and does not guarantee the accuracy, legality, or appropriateness of the output.

Please use the models at your own judgment and responsibility for any consequences that arise from their use. Please refrain from using them to attack third parties, infringe on rights, or for any purpose that violates the law.

04

Handling of Demos and Data

Demos hosted on Hugging Face Spaces do not permanently store input data. Processing is generally intended to be self-contained within a session; for aspects that depend on Hugging Face's own infrastructure specifications, please also review Hugging Face's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

05

Disclaimer of Warranty

The models, tools, and demos introduced on this site are experimental works produced through independent development. No warranty, express or implied, is provided, and no responsibility is assumed for the continuity of operation or the accuracy of results. Thank you for your understanding.

06

Contact

For questions regarding licensing or usage policy, or inquiries from rights holders, please contact me via X (@ek15072809) or GitHub.
